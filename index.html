Food for the mind, body & soul

Big, colourful and packed to the rafters since 1988

Vegie Bar is a buzzing Brunswick Street warehouse that's been feeding Melbourne meat-free for more than three decades. The menu roams from Italian to Japanese and South-East Asia, and we're happy to look after gluten-free, dairy-free and raw diets.

Grab a cocktail at the bar while you wait, and finish with vegan ice cream from our sister venue Girls & Boys, right next door.

What sorts of vegetarian food are we talking about here?

We've got a mainstay of vegetarian ( & vegan) dishes that people just keep coming back for like the original style Mostly Greens and our Laksa. But realistically we are talking about food from all corners of the globe... Italian is strongly represented (pizzas / oven baked lasagne / gnocchi) alongside Japanese and Asian inspired dishes such as our Zen Bowls & Mee Goreng. We're serving a full range of cocktails as well as serving legendary vegan ice cream from our sister restaurant Girls & Boys next door.

How do I get to Vegie Bar on public transport?

You can get to Vegie Bar easily on the tram or bus using the following routes:

  • Tram Route 11 along Brunswick Street, get off at Stop 17 & you're at Vegie Bar!
  • Tram Route 96 along Nicholson St, get off at Stop 14 & walk 300m east along Rose St
  • Tram Route 86 along Smith St, get off at Stop 20 & walk 500m west along Rose St
  • Bus Routes 200, 202 & 207 along Johnston St, get off at Brunswick St & walk 280m north

Where can I park?

The easiest place for parking is the Fitzroy Mills Carpark @ 75 Rose Street, Fitzroy - just 50m from our front door

Metered parking is available along Brunswick Street

Metered and unmetered 1P & 2P parking is available in all local side streets

What makes Vegie Bar one of the Best Melbourne Vegan / Vegetarian restaurants?

Vegie Bar restaurant has been a cornerstone of plant-based dining in Melbourne since 1988, offering a diverse, flavour-packed menu of vegan and vegetarian dishes. Whether you’re visiting for hearty vegan burgers, globally inspired curries, or decadent vegan desserts, our food is crafted with fresh, natural ingredients. As a leading Melbourne vegan restaurant, we pride ourselves on inclusivity—offering options for gluten-free, dairy-free, and raw diets too.

Is Vegie Bar a good choice if I’m looking for Melbourne vegetarian restaurant options?

Absolutely. Vegie Bar is both vegetarian and vegan friendly. Even though some dishes are fully vegan, others include vegetarian ingredients, allowing diners to choose according to their dietary preferences. If you’re searching for a Melbourne vegetarian restaurant, you’ll find plenty here—from cheesy (including vegan cheese) pizzas and salads to rich vegetarian mains—all created with quality, ethical ingredients.

How does Vegie Bar compare to other Vegan Eateries in Melbourne?

Among vegan eateries in Melbourne, Vegie Bar stands out for its extensive menu (50+ dishes plus daily specials), generous portions, and vibrant atmosphere. We aim to offer excellent value—delicious food that doesn’t compromise on creativity or quality. Our Brunswick St location is a full vibe, making us a favourite for both locals and visitors looking to enjoy vegan food in Melbourne.
